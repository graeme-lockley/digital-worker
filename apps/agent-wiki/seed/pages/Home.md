## Structure

Pages are organised by **slug path** in the sidebar tree. Keep each page **relatively small**; split large topics into child pages and link with wiki syntax.

- **Top-level pages** — e.g. `Home`, [Usage|Usage]
- **Nested paths** — e.g. [people/graeme|Example operator] under the `people` folder

Slugs map to files: `people/graeme` → `pages/people/graeme.md`.

Link between pages: `[slug|label]` or `[[slug|label]]` (see [Usage|Usage] for full rules).

## Who writes here

Agents via `wiki_write`. Operators via this UI. Every edit records `updated_by` and bumps `revision`.
